Historical Options Backtesting

NOT VALIDATED
Backtest integrity requirement: no historical results are generated until a suitable point-in-time options dataset is supplied. Underlying OHLC data alone is not enough to backtest option premiums, IV, Greeks, bid/ask fills, expiry, lot-size changes and slippage accurately.

Dataset requirements

  • Timestamped historical option-chain snapshots or contract-level OHLC/trades.
  • Historical spot/index price synchronized to option timestamps.
  • Expiry, strike, CE/PE, lot-size and contract-master history.
  • Bid/ask spreads, volume and open interest where available.
  • IV/Greeks provenance or enough data to reproduce calculations.
  • Transaction costs, taxes, slippage and realistic fill rules.
  • Delisted/expired contracts retained to avoid survivorship bias.

Validation protocol

  1. Validate timestamps, missing rows and contract identity.
  2. Separate development, validation and untouched out-of-sample periods.
  3. Prevent look-ahead and expiry-selection leakage.
  4. Run walk-forward tests with realistic costs and fills.
  5. Report CAGR, drawdown, Sharpe, hit rate, expectancy and turnover.
  6. Paper trade prospectively before any live approval.
